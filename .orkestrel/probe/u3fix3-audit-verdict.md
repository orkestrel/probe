## The design decision

The fixed resident set plus one recycled slot is the right bound. It preserves declared-project services, admits an undeclared project without letting caller strings increase resident memory, and gives repeated use of one undeclared project useful locality. An LRU adds policy and permits eviction mistakes without improving the stated invariant. Refusing undeclared projects would remove an admitted capability.

Alternating 2 undeclared projects rebuilt services measured at 432–573 ms each. That cost is acceptable for an exceptional undeclared path, but it must remain documented because it permits caller-driven CPU work. Creating and disposing every undeclared service without caching is simpler and reaches the same memory bound, but it also rebuilds repeated use of one project. The one-slot optimization earns its small amount of state.
